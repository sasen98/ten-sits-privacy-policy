Ten Sits Privacy Policy

  • Effective date: 23 October 2026
  • Developer: Apollo Innovation
  • Contact: innovationapollo@gmail.com

Short version: Ten Sits collects nothing. Your practice stays on your phone.

1. What we collect

Nothing. Ten Sits has no account, sign-up or login. It has no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs and no crash reporting, and we run no servers for the app.

2. Network

Ten Sits doesn't request internet access and makes no network connections. The Android app declares no internet permission. All narration, bells and content are built into the app.

After the final day, the app shows an optional link to an external website about residential meditation courses. Tapping it opens your web browser, and that website is governed by its own privacy policy. Ten Sits is not affiliated with it.

3. What stays on your phone

  • Your sessions: for each sit of a minute or more, the date and time, how long you sat, whether it was a guided day or a self-timed sit, and which day.
  • Program progress: which of the ten days you've completed.
  • Settings: your chosen voice, reminder on/off and time, and your last timer settings.

On Android, this data may be included in your phone's own backup to your Google account, if you have device backup turned on, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is managed by Google under your account; we can't see it. Uninstalling the app deletes everything from the phone, and Settings → Delete all data removes it at any time.

Ten Sits does not use your microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

5. This website

This page is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address and browser type when you visit, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The Ten Sits app itself never contacts this website or any other server.

6. Children

Ten Sits doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data.

7. Health

Ten Sits offers general information about meditation. It is not a medical device and not a substitute for medical or mental-health care.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date.
