Ten Sits

A gentle 10-day introduction to Vipassana-style meditation.

Ten Sits is for anyone who'd like to learn meditation but can't get away for a long retreat. One guided sit a day, a little longer each time, with a calm voice that teaches you what to do and then gives you silence.

Anapana · days 1–4
Awareness of the breath, to calm the mind and steady your attention.
Vipassana · days 5–9
A body scan: seeing sensations clearly, without reacting to them.
Metta · day 10
Closing with goodwill towards yourself and others.

Help

The audio stops when I lock my phone

Ten Sits keeps playing with the screen off. If it stops, check that battery saver isn't restricting the app: Settings → Apps → Ten Sits → Battery → Unrestricted (Android).

I don't get the daily reminder

Turn it on in the app's Settings and allow notifications when asked. Reminders can arrive a few minutes late; that's how Android saves battery.

Can I go back to an earlier day?

Yes. Open All ten days and tap any day you've already sat. You can also restart the whole program; your total time is kept.

Something isn't working

Email innovationapollo@gmail.com with your phone model and what happened.

A note on wellbeing

Meditation is usually calming, but it can occasionally bring up difficult feelings. It's always fine to stop. Ten Sits is not a substitute for medical or mental-health care.
